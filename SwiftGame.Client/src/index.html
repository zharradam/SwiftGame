Swiftology: the Taylor Swift song quiz

How well do you know Taylor Swift? Hear a short clip of one of her songs, then pick the right title from four choices. You have 10 seconds, and the faster you answer, the more points you score, up to 10,000 a song.

Ways to play

  • Normal: 10 random songs from every era.
  • Daily Swiftle: the same 10 songs for everyone each day, with streaks and a daily leaderboard.
  • Album mode: quiz yourself on the eras you choose.
  • Which album?: hear a song and name its era.
  • Challenge a friend: send a link and they play your exact songs.

Every era

Taylor Swift, Fearless, Speak Now, Red, 1989, reputation, Lover, folklore, evermore, Midnights, The Tortured Poets Department and The Life of a Showgirl, including Taylor's Versions and From The Vault tracks.

Free to play in your browser. Sign up to put your name on the live leaderboard, earn friendship-bracelet beads and pick your favourite era's look.
